WHY ZA EXISTS

The gap between universities
and the people they need to reach.

ZA started after the rapid growth of private and national/private universities in Egypt over the last three years. The founders identified a communication gap between these universities and the audiences they need to reach.

Having recently experienced university life themselves, they saw first-hand how university communication can fail to translate the real experience into content — and built ZA to close that gap through strategic, high-quality content grounded in a university's identity, stories, people, facilities and community.

  • 01New universities need awareness, differentiation and credibility — all at once.
  • 02They speak to prospective students, parents, transfer & international students, current students and the wider community.
  • 03Valuable stories — facilities, programs, faculty, leadership — often go unrepresented in content.
  • 04Without a clear content strategy, production becomes event-led instead of audience-led.

HOW ZA DELIVERS

SERVICES

Our process and the services it produces — research before production, every time.

OUR PROCESS

Nine phases. Research before production, every time.

01

Research

Client brief, meeting notes, website / brand research, social media audit and content inventory.

02

Diagnosis

Content mix analysis, audience analysis, and funnel / decision-journey mapping.

03

Insight

Creative, hook and storytelling review, plus visual identity and brand-voice review where source material allows.

04

Strategy

Competitor benchmarking, gap analysis, opportunity identification and brand / content strategy.

05

Creative Direction

Content architecture that carries the strategy into a clear creative direction.

06

Pre‑Production

Production calendar and plan, and all groundwork ahead of the shoot.

07

Production

On-the-ground production of the agreed content assets.

08

Post‑Production

Editing, finishing and refinement of every asset.

09

Final Delivery

Final assets, delivered and ready for the university to use.
